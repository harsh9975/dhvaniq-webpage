Questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about setting up and deploying Dhvaniq voice assistants.

How does Dhvaniq know how to answer questions about my business?

Dhvaniq is grounded exclusively in the business knowledge you provide—including PDF manuals, service lists, operating hours, and booking guidelines. When a caller asks a question, Dhvaniq searches your indexed documents in real time and formulates an answer derived directly from those sources, helping you review answers against your source material.

Can I keep my existing business phone number?

Yes. You do not need to change carriers or port your phone number. You can configure simple conditional call-forwarding through your existing provider (such as forwarding calls when your line is busy, after 4 rings, or after business hours) directly to your assigned Dhvaniq assistant number.

What happens if a caller asks something outside the assistant’s knowledge?

Dhvaniq follows strict fallback guidelines. Instead of guessing, it politely informs the caller that it doesn't have that specific information, offers to capture their contact details and inquiry, and notifies your staff for direct follow-up.

How do appointment bookings and captured requests get confirmed?

Dhvaniq captures the caller’s requested service, preferred date, and preferred time window. It flags the request as “Awaiting Team Confirmation” and instantly alerts your staff via SMS, email, or your CRM dashboard, allowing your team to review and confirm the booking with full oversight.

Can I test and preview conversations before going live?

Yes. Dhvaniq Studio includes an interactive testing simulator where you can talk to or text your assistant directly in your browser. You can test edge cases, refine responses, review source citations, and publish updates only when completely satisfied.

Is caller information secure and confidential?

Customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Dhvaniq employs strict data isolation and redaction protocols so caller recordings and personal information are accessible solely by authorized administrators on your account.
